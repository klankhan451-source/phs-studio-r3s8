Advanced — your own pictures & words

Optional, for later: give any reading word your own picture — a photo of your pet, your classroom, your shop mascot. Your picture is then used everywhere that word appears in the book. Pictures are kept in this browser, inside your saved projects. You do not need any of this to make a great book.

Add your own word (up to 3 — with its own picture)

Your word can appear in sentences like any other word. It must only use the sounds taught up to this level, so beginners can still sound it out — if it doesn’t fit, we’ll tell you and it won’t be added.

Step 2 — Pick activities

Tick the games and practice pages to put inside the book, and say how many of each. The numbers already filled in make a great first book — you can leave them exactly as they are. Every sentence uses only this level’s sounds, so a beginner can read every word.

Word theme (optional — make most words about one topic, like animals or food)

Advanced — your own sentences

Optional, for later: type your own sentences, one per line (up to 150). Add | and an emoji after a sentence to choose its picture — for example “The cat is on the roof. | 🐱”. The two spare pictures are picked for you, from the same group. Without an emoji, the first reading word in the sentence becomes the picture. A sentence whose picture can’t be matched becomes a Read & Draw page instead.

Check & edit the pages

Want to change something? Rewrite any sentence here, or pick a different picture — the two spare pictures update to stay in the same group (all animals, all food…). The answer key is made from exactly what you see here, so it always matches the book. Nothing to change? Just go on to Step 3.

How it works

  • Real reading: every sentence uses only the sounds taught up to the chosen level, plus a few “learn-by-heart” words. Each level keeps all the sounds from the levels before it.
  • No guessing: the 3 pictures on a strip always come from the same group (all animals, all food…), so the child must read the word — the pictures can’t give it away.
  • Answer keys are made from the printed pages themselves — change any page and the key changes with it.
  • Selling your books? Your licence covers selling the finished reading books you create (see the Commercial Use Licence in your download). The tool and your code themselves must never be shared or resold.
  • Print: use “Print / Save PDF” at 100% size. For Amazon KDP, make the book here first, then make its matching cover in the KDP cover maker (Step 3, near the bottom).
