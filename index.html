Add your own word (up to 3 — with its own picture)

Your word joins this level’s reading words, so it can appear in sentences and strips like any other word. To keep the decodable promise, the word must only use the sounds taught up to this level — if it doesn’t fit, we’ll tell you and it won’t be added.

Activities

Tick the activities to include and set how many items each one gets. Every sentence is checked against the level — only this level’s sounds and focus words are used. The three pictures on each strip always come from the same group, so children must read, not guess.

Your own sentences

Optional: type your own sentences, one per line (up to 150). Add | emoji after a sentence to choose its picture — the two near-miss pictures are picked automatically from the same group. Without an emoji, the first reading word found in the sentence becomes the picture. Sentences whose picture cannot be matched become Read & Draw pages.

Content editor

Every generated item can be changed here: rewrite any sentence, and for picture strips pick a different picture — the near-miss pictures update to stay in the same group. Answer keys are rebuilt from exactly what is shown here, so they can never disagree with the book.

How it works

  • Decodable promise: every generated sentence uses only the chosen level’s phonics patterns plus its Focus Words (sight words). Levels are cumulative — each level keeps all earlier sounds.
  • Near-miss pictures: the 3 pictures on a strip always come from the same group (all animals, all food…), so the child must read the word, not guess by picture type.
  • Answer keys are generated from the printed pages themselves — edit any item and the key updates with it.
  • Selling your books? Your licence covers selling the finished reading books you create (see the Commercial Use Licence in your download). The tool and your code themselves must never be shared or resold.
  • Print: use “Print / Save PDF” at 100% scale. For KDP, build the interior here, then make the matching wrap cover in the KDP Wrap Cover Builder.
